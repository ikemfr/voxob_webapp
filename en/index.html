How to use VoxoB — make voxel art for free

VoxoB is a voxel editor that runs entirely in your browser. Make 3D pixel art and voxel models on a computer, tablet or phone. No install, no sign-up. Your work is never uploaded; it is saved in the browser you are using.

Make voxel art in 3 steps

  1. Make a shape: In the Make tab on the right, generate a house, castle, tree, terrain and more with one click, or place voxels yourself with the pen and rectangle tools on the left.
  2. Finish it: Press ✨ Next move to add ground, a roof, windows, shading and more, then recolour everything with Style or Palette.
  3. Export: Use Export at the top right to save OBJ, STL, GLB, PNG or video, ready for 3D printing, game assets or social posts.

Getting started

On your first visit you can pick: generate something, start from an empty canvas, or make it from a picture.

On a computer, pick a tool on the left (pen, line, rectangle, circle, fill…) and click the 3D view or the 2D slice. Right-drag to orbit, Shift-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

On a phone, open the panels from the bottom bar or Tools and Settings. Tap to place, two fingers to turn, pinch to zoom.

Turn pictures, text and video into cities and creatures (Convert)

In the Make tab on the right, open Convert, pick an image or a video or type some text, choose a style and press ✨ Convert. It all happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

City reads the picture as a map: dark or vivid parts grow into tall buildings, green into parks, blue into water, and from straight above the roofs show the picture. Creature cuts out the outline and puffs it up, thicker where the shape is wider. Tower turns the outline around like a potter’s wheel. Terrain makes brightness into height. There are also Relief, Flat and, for video, Time sculpture, which stacks whatever moves frame by frame into a solid trail.

With “Keep the conversion process as animation” on, the build-up becomes frames. 📱 Shorts (9:16) records a vertical video: the source, the conversion, then a turn around the result.

Use pictures, videos and text you made yourself or have the rights to, and avoid other people’s faces and works.

Next move and styles

Press ✨ Next move at the top (or N) and VoxoB adds the next sensible step to what you have: ground, a roof, windows, trees, shading or darker edges, only the ones that fit the shape. Each can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

Style in the right panel recolours the whole model at once: Japanese, retro, pastel, night, autumn or mono. Palette offers sets such as traditional Japanese colours and a retro 16-colour set.

Animation

Add frames and build a flip-book animation. Animation generators in the right panel turn the current shape into 26 kinds of motion: spin, swing, grow, dissolve and more.

Set the speed with FPS and export WebM or MP4 video.

From images, video and 3D models

Load PNG / JPG / GIF / BMP images, MP4 / WebM / MOV video, or OBJ / STL models and VoxoB turns them into voxels. Images can get depth from their colours, handy for turning pixel art into 3D.

Export and save

Export OBJ (+MTL), STL for 3D printing, GLB for game engines and web 3D, PNG, WebM / MP4 video, or JSON.

Work is saved in the browser automatically, and you can keep several named projects. Share packs your work into a URL (nothing is stored on a server).

What people make with it

  • Models for 3D printing (STL export)
  • Game assets and icons for Blender, Unity, Godot and more (OBJ / GLB)
  • Block-built houses, towns and Japanese shrines and pagodas
  • Turntable animations and short clips for social media (MP4 / WebM)
  • Learning 3D and spatial shapes at school or home, even on devices where you cannot install apps

FAQ

Is it free?
Yes. No sign-up either.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Open the page in a browser such as Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. A device with WebGL is required.
Where is my data stored?
Only in your browser (localStorage). Clearing browser data removes it, so export important work as JSON.
Does it work on phones?
Yes. Tap to place, two fingers to turn, pinch to zoom. It works on iPhone, Android and iPad.
Can I use my models in Blender or Unity?
Yes. Export OBJ (+MTL) or GLB and import it into Blender, Unity, Godot and other 3D tools and game engines.
Can I 3D print my model?
Yes. Export STL and open it in your slicer (Cura, PrusaSlicer and so on).
Can it convert an image to voxels?
Yes. Load a PNG / JPG / GIF / BMP and it becomes voxels, optionally with depth from its colours. Video and OBJ / STL models can be converted too.
How is it different from MagicaVoxel?
VoxoB runs in the browser with nothing to install, and works on phones. One-click generators and Next move give you a shape without placing every voxel by hand.
